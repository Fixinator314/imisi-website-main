Discover

What exists?

Exposure comes first.

Young people encounter museums, collections, heritage sites, artisans, laboratories and the professionals who work within them.

Museum Discovery Days School Outreach Behind-the-Scenes Tours Meet-the-Artisan Documentary Screenings
02

Connect

How do these worlds intersect?

Different ways of knowing meet around the same material.

Artisans, scientists, museum professionals, researchers and communities reveal different perspectives on materials, knowledge and heritage.

Materials Disciplines Experts Living Knowledge Scientific Knowledge
03

Learn

Can I do this?

Curiosity becomes structured education.

Learners move into structured programmes that introduce museums, archaeology, paleontology, geology, anthropology, material culture, craft, conservation, photography and storytelling.

Museum Academy Master Artisan Workshops Field Schools Youth Mentorship
04

Experience

What does this feel like?

Learning enters the real world.

Learners gain direct exposure to excavations, fossil sites, museum laboratories, craft studios, communities and protected environments.

Excavations Fossil Sites Museum Labs Craft Studios Communities
05

Create

What can I contribute?

Knowledge becomes output.

Participants turn what they have learned into tangible work — creating products, businesses, documentaries, exhibitions, archives and research.

Products Businesses Mini Documentaries Exhibitions Archives
06

Transition

Where do I go next?

Learning becomes opportunity.

Participants are connected to pathways beyond the programme — further study, internships, assistantships, apprenticeships and enterprise.

University Internships Assistantships Apprenticeships Entrepreneurship
07

Return

How do I give back?

The journey becomes self-renewing.

Graduates return to the ecosystem as mentors, guest speakers, facilitators and contributors — helping create the next generation of participants.

Mentor Guest Speaker Facilitator Contributor

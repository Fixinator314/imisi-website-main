    02 Why This Matters

    Heritage is not a relic. It is infrastructure.

    Across Africa, extraordinary knowledge exists inside communities, institutions and living traditions. Yet too often, the pathways connecting that knowledge to the next generation are fragmented.

    Ìmísí Foundation exists to build those pathways — connecting knowledge holders, learners, institutions and opportunity through a continuous journey of discovery, learning, practice and return.

    03 Two Knowledge Systems

    Living knowledge. Formal knowledge.

    Two systems. One pathway.

    01
    Living Knowledge

    Knowledge carried by people.

    Knowledge that lives inside communities, families, practitioners and knowledge holders. It is carried through experience, practice, memory and participation.

    Oral tradition Craft & making Community practice Intergenerational knowledge
    02
    Formal Knowledge

    Knowledge held by institutions.

    Knowledge preserved, researched and developed through museums, universities, archives, laboratories and cultural institutions.

    Research Archives Museums Academic study

    Ìmísí connects both systems — allowing knowledge to move between people, institutions and generations.

    04 The Ecosystem

    Knowledge doesn't move alone.

    It moves through people, places and institutions — each playing a different role in keeping heritage alive.

    01
    Knowledge Holders

    The people who carry the knowledge.

    Practitioners, artisans, elders, researchers and cultural custodians who hold knowledge and pass it forward.

    02
    Learners

    The next generation.

    Young people discovering, learning, practising and transforming knowledge into new possibilities.

    03
    Institutions

    Places where knowledge lives.

    Museums, universities, archives, cultural organisations and other institutions that preserve and develop knowledge.

    04
    Communities

    Where knowledge returns.

    Communities provide the context, identity and living environment that give knowledge meaning.

    05
    Opportunity

    Where knowledge becomes possibility.

    Industry, employment, enterprise and further study create pathways for knowledge to become sustainable opportunity.

    When these relationships work together, heritage becomes a living system.
